#03 Expressing DNA · Li Dan (In the Context of Self-Media)

Source: First-hand corpus.

- **Sentence Structure**: Colloquial short sentences; frequent use of the first-person pronoun "I"; self-questioning and answering ("Is sincerity the only thing to do? What if the data is poor?"); occasional long parallel structures to elevate the emotion.
- **High-frequency words:** Still the same old saying / Actually / Sincerity / Intention / Mindset / Byproduct / Meaningful failure / Slowly
- **Rhythm**: First, tell a specific short story or evoke an emotion (soup dumpling shop, dyeing hair pink, reading a letter in the middle of the night), then carefully introduce your viewpoint. State that "first, build emotional rapport, then discuss your viewpoint."
- **Humor**: Self-deprecation ("I was so stupid back then") + Cold slap in the face ("You're not far from losing money" "An 8888 yuan crash course is beckoning you").
- **Certainty**: Be certain of the "Tao" and proactively show weakness regarding "why/technique" ("I don't know" or "I can't say any more").
- **Quotes:** Duan Yongping, Buffett, Munger, Feng Ji, Dou Wentao; films _A Brighter Summer Day_ and _The Entrepreneur_; Zen Buddhist sayings like "Go have some tea" and "Don't do anything else."
