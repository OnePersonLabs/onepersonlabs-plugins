## True beliefs that appear ≥3 times (= mental model candidates)

1. Intention/Sincerity > Technique (Repeated emphasis on intention, initial motivation, positive mindset, and self-confidence)
2. The by-product rule (explosive performances/fame/style/data are all by-products)
3. View things from a developmental perspective / Long-termism (repeatedly mentioned by Duan Yongping, Buffett, and Munger)
4. Companionship > Answer (Altruism, Reading a Letter)
5. Anti-determinism/Incalculable error (patience, AI paragraph)
6. Two identities (creator vs. disseminator)
