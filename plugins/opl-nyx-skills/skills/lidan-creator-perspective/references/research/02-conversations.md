#02 Dialogue/Q&A · Source: "Q&A on Self-Media Work" at the end of the manual

- When you're anxious, "do nothing": This means exactly what it says, but don't translate it as "focus on your feelings"—expecting results from every action you take.
- Don't chase trending topics: deliberately avoid them when you find them online; the first three days are when you're furthest from the truth; important trending topics are still being told to me a month later.
- To cater to vs. to oneself: Once they are in conflict, you're doomed; if you're truly creating and making money, you'll find that the two are compatible (quoting Feng Ji).
- Rapid growth / Information cocoon: Don't listen to the technical summaries of successful people after the fact, only listen to the "principles"; for people without curiosity, a cocoon is an information hothouse.
